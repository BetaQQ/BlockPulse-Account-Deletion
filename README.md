# Delete your BlockPulse account and data

This page explains how to request deletion of your BlockPulse account and associated cloud game data, including if you have already uninstalled the app.

## Delete from the app

Open BlockPulse and go to **Settings > Delete account**. Confirm the request. BlockPulse will delete your Firebase Authentication account, cloud profile, campaign progress, and leaderboard entry. This deletes your BlockPulse account only; it does **not** delete your Google account.

## Request deletion without the app

If you cannot access BlockPulse, request deletion by email from the same Google account address that you used to sign in to BlockPulse. Include your BlockPulse display name so we can locate your account. **Do not send your password.** We will verify the request and delete the account and active BlockPulse cloud data. Copies held temporarily in service-provider backups may be removed under their retention processes or legal requirements.

[**Email a BlockPulse account deletion request**](mailto:syokusoglu1@gmail.com?subject=BlockPulse%20account%20deletion&body=Please%20delete%20my%20BlockPulse%20account%20and%20associated%20cloud%20game%20data.%0A%0ABlockPulse%20display%20name%3A%20)

You can also email **[syokusoglu1@gmail.com](mailto:syokusoglu1@gmail.com?subject=BlockPulse%20account%20deletion)**. The email address is obtained from Google sign-in; BlockPulse does not ask you to create a separate password or provide another email address.

## Guest play

Guest play does not create an online BlockPulse account. Guest progress is stored on your device. Clear BlockPulse app storage or uninstall the app to remove it.
