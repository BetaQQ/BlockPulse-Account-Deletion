Delete your BlockPulse account

You can request deletion of your BlockPulse account and associated cloud game data. This removes your Firebase Authentication account, BlockPulse cloud profile, campaign progress, and leaderboard entry. It does not delete your Google account.

Delete from the app

Open BlockPulse, go to Settings > Delete account, and confirm the request.

Request deletion without the app

If you cannot access BlockPulse, send an email from the Google account address you used to sign in. Include your BlockPulse display name so we can locate the account. Do not send your password. We will verify the request and delete the account and active BlockPulse cloud data. Copies held temporarily in service-provider backups may be removed under their retention processes or legal requirements.

Email a deletion request

Or email syokusoglu1@gmail.com.

Guest play

Guest play does not create an online BlockPulse account. Guest progress is stored on your device. Clear BlockPulse app storage or uninstall the app to remove it.
